About LawVM

LawVM compiles legal state from amendment acts. It demonstrates that covered recurring forms of human-written amendment law can be compiled into typed text-state instructions. For a declared source/profile and selected date, it can derive covered provision text, identify the act that changed it, and expose where the result is uncertain.

The assurance boundary is narrow: text-state derivation from the available sources, with explicit evidence for what was observed, inferred, recovered, and left unresolved. Source completeness, legislator intent, comparison-surface authority, and broader doctrinal interpretation remain outside that claim.
