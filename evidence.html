After a finding

Evidence first; notification and publication follow an agreed process

  1. Freeze the record. Preserve the declared sources, implementation snapshot, command, candidate state, comparison surface, and alternative explanations.
  2. Notify a named reviewer. Send the candidate through the channel agreed with the source keeper or institution; do not publish restricted material or treat silence as confirmation.
  3. Record a disposition. Confirmed, refuted, LawVM defect, source pathology, blocked, unresolved, or another declared status remains attached to the evidence.
  4. Disclose only what is permitted. Publish a case only after its evidence, disposition, notification boundary, and permissions support public review.
No automatic action: LawVM does not correct an official text, notify a publisher, or publish a candidate on its own.

What a public case should contain

Precise claim

Jurisdiction, legal work, address, time scope, claimed difference, and maximum permissible conclusion.

Source chain

Source amendment, before witness, candidate state, official comparison surface, locators, and hashes where publishable.

Reproduction

Software and source snapshots, profile, command, rule IDs, residuals, and downloadable artifacts.

Disposition

Candidate, confirmed, refuted, LawVM defect, source pathology, blocked, or representative clean transition—with dates.
