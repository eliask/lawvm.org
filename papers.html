Paper in preparation

Finland replay compiler in preparation

Working title: Reconstructing Point-in-Time Statutory State from Amendment Streams: A Replay Compiler for Finnish Law

LawVM replays Finnish amendment streams into point-in-time text-state, compares the result with a declared consolidation witness, and classifies residual mismatches instead of collapsing them into one error bucket. An August 2026 development run reported 0.23% mean normalized text edit distance. That result is project-reported, not an error or correctness percentage.

Status: drafting in progress. The public evidence ledger reports the Finnish candidate work only in aggregate while external disposition is pending.

Public wording: the current assurance pages, evidence records, and dated results define the public claim ceiling. Draft paper language stays within that ceiling.
