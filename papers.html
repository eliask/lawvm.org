Paper in preparation

Finland replay compiler in preparation

Working title: Reconstructing Point-in-Time Statutory State from Amendment Streams: A Replay Compiler for Finnish Law

LawVM replays Finnish amendment streams into point-in-time text-state, compares the result with a declared consolidation witness, and classifies residual mismatches instead of collapsing them into one error bucket. An August 2026 development run reported 0.23% mean normalized text edit distance. That result is project-reported, not an error or correctness percentage, and the exact run package is not public.

Status: drafting in progress. Internal corpus evidence exists, but the frozen source, corpus manifest, per-row output, environment, and citable public release needed for independent reproduction remain unavailable. The public evidence ledger reports the Finnish candidate work only in aggregate while external disposition is pending.

Public wording: the current site registries and dated snapshot define the public claim ceiling. Draft paper language does not widen it.
