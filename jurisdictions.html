How to compare frontends

The axes that matter

Source

Are official artifacts inventoried, archived reproducibly, and complete for a declared subset?

Structure and effects

Is source structure loss-preserving? Are amendment effects merely candidates, typed, dry-run checked, or promoted?

Replay and point-in-time state

Is mutation unauthorized, shadow-only, bounded and promoted, or broad? Can point-in-time state be materialized?

Verification

Are there contract tests, independent before/after witnesses, corpus accounting, or an externally confirmed disposition?
