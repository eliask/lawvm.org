Read a result correctly

An invariant narrows a result; it does not certify the law.

An invariant is a named property checked over a declared source bundle, profile, time scope, and frontend. A result is meaningful only with its disposition and enforcement mode.

UK example: the current UK lane is a bounded workbench with explicit source, manual-compilation, and comparison limits. Its current diagnostics are not a universal publication-blocking gate or broad official-consolidation claim.

Source readiness is a prerequisite: if source identity, closure, or coverage is missing, the correct result is a blocked readiness account, not a clean replay. LawVM returns read-only evidence; it does not publish or automatically correct official text.

Seven questions

How a legal text-state claim earns its wording

source identity→declared universe→typed operation→phase-local admission→observed mutation→temporal state→bounded checker
01 / SOURCE

Which exact artifacts were used?

Canonical identity, manifestations, availability, roles, and roots can bind a claim to declared inputs. Hashes establish identity and tamper evidence—not source correctness.

02 / ACCOUNT

Did anything silently disappear?

A checked universe partitions effects into emitted operations, typed rejections, typed observations, residuals, or violations. Conservation cannot prove the universe itself was complete.

03 / MEANING

What typed operation crossed the boundary?

Closure requires named target, payload, source anchor, time, and family-specific fields. It does not independently prove that arbitrary legislative prose entailed that operation.

04 / ADMISSION

Why could it execute?

Evidence and confidence are not portable permissions. A scoped phase must validate the exact operation and state head. This enforcement is not yet uniform across frontends.

05 / MUTATION

What actually changed?

A receipt records writer testimony; a separate tree diff can observe the landed footprint and compare it with the declared target and named allowances.

06 / TIME

Why is this the selected state?

Effective time, expiry, temporary overlays, occupancy, identity, branch, and authority regime are distinct. Formal models cover named abstractions, not complete jurisdictional doctrine.

07 / CHECK

What can another program reconstruct?

Experimental read-only checkers validate bounded structural roots, links, and declared transition folding. No stable public legal-certificate product is claimed.

RESIDUAL HONESTY

What remains blocked or unresolved?

Missing proof remains blocked, unresolved, or uncheckable; a contradiction is invalid; work outside the profile is not claimed. A clean-looking text cannot erase the account.

Future strict authoring

A conditional compiler theorem, not a present certification.

Strict authoring is a future direction for legislation represented in a formally specified, machine-checkable language.

For a theorem, the dossier would need a frozen and complete authoritative source bundle; correspondence between human-approved text and its machine artifact; formal grammar, semantics, and rejection rules; sound and complete lowering to typed operations; exact target, identity, and temporal rules; replay and materialization refinement; end-to-end implementation evidence; an independently implemented checker; intact content-addressed artifacts; live guards; and no blocking residuals.

Conditional theorem: under those conditions, a clean dossier could establish that generated text-state matches the declared formal semantics for every accepted input in its jurisdiction, profile, and time query. Unsupported or ambiguous input would be rejected or blocked. The institution would retain authority over source status, legal judgment, and publication.

Current nonclaim: LawVM does not yet provide that end-to-end source-language entailment or implementation-refinement proof. Current frontends and checks therefore remain scoped, qualified, residual-preserving, or observe-only where their profiles say so.
