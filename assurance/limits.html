Explicit non-claims

LawVM does not currently prove

Global source completeness

A complete account of declared inputs cannot prove that no legally relevant source exists outside the declared horizon.

Source-keeper correctness

A valid hash proves identity, not that XML, HTML, PDF, OCR, or metadata expresses the legally correct publication.

General source-to-operation entailment

No general independent checker yet proves arbitrary legislative prose entails the canonical operation emitted by a frontend.

Legal interpretation or authority

LawVM does not prove doctrine, constitutional validity, application to facts, legislative intent, or which institution has legal authority.

End-to-end formal verification

Z3 and TLA+ work covers named abstractions. There is no complete model-to-Python refinement proof or whole-system formal audit.

Uniform frontend enforcement

Receipt, mutation-audit, admission, PIT, and certificate maturity differ by frontend, operation family, profile, and corpus.

A stable certificate product

The checker and bundle machinery is experimental; schemas, ownership, compatibility, CLI, and release packaging are not frozen.

Comparison-surface infallibility

Agreement with an official or editorial consolidation is corroboration. Disagreement is a review question, not an automatic verdict.

Missing-artifact rule: when a required committed artifact is unavailable, the correct outcome is UNCHECKABLE—not an inferred pass, failure, or reconstructed substitute.

The largest open assurance gap

Independently recheck the source-to-operation step.

The highest-value core-repository work is a narrow independent entailment checker for one operation family at a time: confirm the exact source span, grammar version, operative verb, target expression, payload, ambiguity state, and emitted operation without invoking the original lowering implementation.

committed source bytes→exact source span→small frozen grammar→target + payload obligations→operation accepted or rejected

This is a roadmap statement, not a current public capability.
