Scan-first rule: OCR supplies candidate text and locators. Characters, reading order, grouping, tables, citations, completeness, documentary authority, and amendment meaning each retain their own evidence and residuals.

Beyond transcription

A scan can become a reviewable evidence package.

A high-assurance recovery workflow can preserve the source, account for every page and region, compare independent observations, retain alternatives and unread material, and emit a deterministic candidate derivative for review. Unsupported units remain unresolved instead of being silently guessed.

Source and page account

Freeze the supplied bytes and package positions; inventory page images, embedded objects, locators, and missing or unreadable material without inventing page artifacts.

Separate evidence lanes

Keep embedded text, OCR, pixels, geometry, layout, source-native structure, and external transcriptions distinct. Agreement is evidence; disagreement is a review route, not an automatic winner.

Claim-specific reconstruction

Characters, reading order, grouping, headings, tables, footnotes, and coverage are different propositions. A strong text result cannot hide unresolved order or structure.

Derivative and review boundary

Bind every candidate unit back to source loci, independently reread the output, preserve alternatives and residuals, and promote only the units allowed by the declared review profile.

Public capability: a bounded source-readiness pilot can produce this documentary evidence package. Legal-source admission and institutional review govern identity, authority, interpretation, temporal effect, and publication.

First bounded assessment

Return a readiness map with next requirements.

Inputs

A representative source sample, publication registers, identifier and citation conventions, known authority rules, and local source expertise.

Measurements

Package/page/image coverage; text, geometry, layout, and structure observations; alternatives and residual regions; independent readback; identity, version linkage, amendment closure, dates, and expressions.

Outputs

Source account, claim-by-claim evidence map, achievable citation granularity, reviewed candidate derivative where supported, blocked-source map, transition feasibility, and next integration boundary.

Decision

What can run immediately, what becomes possible with named additional sources, and what remains unavailable.
