Current boundary: LawVM is not offered as a generic OCR service. Scanned-source work is presently a feasibility and evidence-quality lane; mature digitisation or publishing platforms can feed structured sources into declared LawVM checks. No scan-first result automatically publishes or corrects legal text.

First bounded assessment

Return a blocked-source map, not false precision.

Inputs

A representative source sample, publication registers, identifier and citation conventions, known authority rules, and local source expertise.

Measurements

Availability, preservation, extraction, structure, identity, version linkage, amendment-source closure, effective dates, and language manifestations.

Outputs

Source census, achievable citation granularity, extraction benchmark, transition feasibility, blocked-source map, and recommended next integration boundary.

Decision

What can run immediately, what becomes possible with named additional sources, and what remains unavailable.
