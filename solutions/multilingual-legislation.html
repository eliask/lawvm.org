For publishers, ministries, and bilingual legal teams

Separate four questions before drawing a conclusion.

Expression state

Each language has its own source, checkpoint, state root, coverage, and residuals. No expression is treated as controlling by default.

Structural pairing

Corresponding chapters, sections, subsections, items, and other units may be exact, one-sided, split/merged, ambiguous, or unpaired.

Parallel QA

Named profiles compare typed facts such as citations, identifiers, dates, symbols, or quantities where the check is appropriate.

Human review

Bilingual legal reviewers disposition candidates, identify legitimate translation shape, and decide what requires institutional action.

Neutral text first; findings on demand.

Local legal review

The institutional output is a concise queue of exact structural or invariant-fact observations, rather than a noisy word-by-word translation diff.

First bounded pilot

One domain, every required expression.

Inputs

All authoritative language manifestations, amendment sources, checkpoints, effective dates, identifiers, and a bilingual legal reviewer.

Outputs

Expression inventory, structural alignment account, invariant-fact findings, unpaired units, exact provenance, and review dispositions.

Non-claims

No automated ruling on semantic equivalence; no default controlling language; no state mutation from a comparison result.

First useful scope

A source-complete transition corpus in one legal domain, with a deliberately narrow and versioned check profile.
