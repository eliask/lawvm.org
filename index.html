LawVM

The law in force should be knowable

Verify changes to the law.

LawVM reconstructs point-in-time legal text from declared amendment sources. It records the operations applied, preserves what could not be proved, and makes disagreements with a published text inspectable.

For publishers and ministries, the practical use is a bounded, no-write review that can surface omitted or misapplied changes, source gaps, and parallel-language inconsistencies without giving software authority to edit the official record.

Start with the artifact you have

Three routes into one evidence model.

Paper and scans call for source bootstrap. A structured export calls for pre-publication QA. A published text with its amendment sources calls for a consolidation audit. Each route keeps its source, model, and uncertainty boundary visible.

declared sources and model→ declared QA checks→ strict or observe-only disposition→ named human decision
Operating boundary: LawVM produces evidence, residuals, and review queues. Here, strict means a declared workflow gate, not legal authority; observe-only records evidence without blocking. LawVM does not automatically correct or publish legal text.

Source bootstrap starts with paper, scans, PDFs, or incomplete archives; pre-publication QA starts with a structured export; consolidation assurance starts with a published text and declared amendment sources.

The mechanism

Compile the transitions behind the latest document.

LawVM preserves the derivation behind a current text: which source changed which legal unit, when it took effect, what mutation was authorized, and what remained unresolved.

source acts→ typed instructions→ target resolution→ replay→ point-in-time state→ evidence + residuals

Independent expressions

In multilingual checks, each declared language expression, source manifestation, and legal-time coordinate keeps its own provenance. Authority remains tied to the declared source for each expression.

Total accounting

Accepted operations, rejected candidates, source pathologies, manual frontiers, and comparison disagreements remain visible.

Mutation boundaries

The mutation account compares the declared target and named allowances with the paths that actually changed. Promotion from observation to blocking varies by frontend.

How it fits

An assurance layer, not a replacement publishing stack.

LawVM can consume versioned artifacts from the systems already used to capture, draft, identify, consolidate, and publish law. Its output is a read-only evidence product for people or CI—not an automatic edit to the official record.

gazette / archive→ AKN / ELI / local XML→ drafting or publisher system→ LawVM shadow assurance→ evidence pack / QA queue / PIT state
Integration boundary: a concrete, versioned adapter is required for each external system. Mentioning a standard or platform does not imply endorsement, certification, or a completed integration.

Frontend portfolio

The frontend registry spans Finland, Estonia, the United Kingdom, New Zealand, the European Union, the United States, Norway, and Sweden, with Japan, Korea, Poland, and Switzerland listed as staging lanes. Each lane has a source-level claim ceiling and an explicit next gate.
